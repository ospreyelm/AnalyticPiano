# Monteverdi Music Strategy

## Target problem

Music theory instructors teaching Bach chorales, part-writing, and voice leading
face a classroom full of students but no way to assess individual work at scale.
Manual grading of chorale homework consumes hours per week — limiting how much
practice students can get and how quickly errors get corrected.

## Our approach

Win by combining a broad importable corpus (Bach, Handel, Clausulae, Partimenti,
and others) with automated real-time grading, so any instructor can build a
curriculum without writing exercises from scratch — and students get instant
correctness feedback without waiting for teacher review.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** Music theory instructor at a university or conservatory — they're
hiring Monteverdi Music to assign and assess chorale and part-writing exercises at
classroom scale without the grading bottleneck.

**Secondary:** The student doing the exercises — they're hiring Monteverdi Music to know
in real time whether their voice-leading is correct.

## Key metrics

- **Exercises completed per student per week** — leading indicator of student
  engagement; measured in DB
- **Teacher time-to-grade** — hours spent on manual grading per assignment cycle;
  qualitative/survey until automated logging exists
- **Skill gap closure rate** — rate at which students advance through tagged skill
  levels; measured via skill tag progression in DB
- **Course adoption** — number of active course sections running on Monteverdi Music;
  measured in DB

## Tracks

### Exercise corpus

Build and maintain the importable library of exercises drawn from canonical
repertoire (Bach, Handel, Clausulae, Partimenti), tagged by theory skill.

_Why it serves the approach:_ The corpus is the prerequisite — automated grading
only scales if the exercise library covers the curriculum.

### Grading infrastructure

Reliable, real-time MIDI grading for all exercise types, with per-note and
per-window correctness feedback.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Grading automation is what closes the feedback loop
that textbooks and PDFs leave open.

### Adaptive engine

Detect student skill gaps from exercise performance data and sequence the right
next exercise.

_Why it serves the approach:_ This converts grading data into curriculum decisions,
replacing per-student teacher intervention.

### Teacher tools

Give instructors the ability to set up courses, assign exercises, and review student
progress without manual grading.

_Why it serves the approach:_ The teacher is the buyer; they need visibility and
control or the product won't get into classrooms.
