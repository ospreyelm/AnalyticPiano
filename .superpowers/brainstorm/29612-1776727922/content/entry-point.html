How should students enter the app?

Three approaches — pick what feels right for your students

A

Unit List (Artusi-style)

Student sees all units in order. Each unit shows a mastery bar and the skills inside it. They click into whichever unit they want and pick an exercise. Simple, student-controlled, no magic.

Pros

  • Transparent — student knows where they are
  • Easy to build
  • Familiar (like a textbook TOC)

Cons

  • Student must decide what to do next
  • No guidance for beginners
B

Auto-Resume + Unit Overview (Khan Academy-style)

Student lands on their current unit. A "Keep going" button resumes exactly where they left off. Below that: unit list with mastery bars so they can jump around. Skill breakdown visible on click.

Pros

  • Zero friction to continue
  • Still shows full picture
  • Research shows highest engagement

Cons

  • More complex to build
  • "Current unit" logic needs defining
C

Skill Map (visual graph)

Student sees a node graph of skills. Locked skills are grayed out. Completed ones glow. They click a skill node to practice it. Like Duolingo's path or a video game skill tree.

Pros

  • Visually motivating
  • Shows dependencies clearly
  • Gamified feel

Cons

  • Complex to build well
  • Can feel overwhelming
  • HarmonyLab already has a skill-map view that could be reused/extended
