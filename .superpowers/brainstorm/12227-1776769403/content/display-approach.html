Question 1: How should notes be displayed?

Unit 1 pitch identification — soprano note naming. Which display approach feels right for beginners?

A — Single voice only

Pros

  • Zero visual clutter
  • Student focuses on single pitch
  • Easier for absolute beginners

Cons

  • No harmonic context
  • Less musical — extracted from real music
B — Full chorale, one voice highlighted

Pros

  • Musical context — hear the harmony
  • Prepares ear for later harmonic units

Cons
